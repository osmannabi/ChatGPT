About

We are 35milimetre, a post-production and design studio in Istanbul. Three people: a compositor with 26 years behind the desk, a graphic designer and a 3D artist. When a job needs more hands, we bring in people we trust. We are not experts in everything, but when we take on a project, we know we can deliver it.

Over the last year we added AI image generation to the pipeline. It is one more plate in the stack, finished by the same hands.
