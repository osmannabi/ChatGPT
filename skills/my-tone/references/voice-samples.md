## 1. Proposal after a video call (KEWE)

> Hi Frantz,
>
> Thank you for taking the time to join the video call today; it was great to hear more about KEWE and where you want to take the brand visually.
>
> I understand you're not happy with the quality of the current renders, and I think that's a fair concern. For a product built around precision and clean energy, the packshots need to feel just as sharp: accurate label artwork, believable materials and clean lighting. So rather than patching the existing images, my proposal is to rebuild them properly from the ground up.
>
> **What we'll do**
> - Model all 5 varieties in 3D: Arctic Blue Razz, Cherry Vanilla, Lemon Lime, Miami Tropical Pulse and Sour Watermelon
> - Apply your label artwork with correct proportions, finishes, and material properties
> - Render each variety on a clean white background, plus a transparent background version (PNG) ready for your website, Amazon listings and marketing materials
>
> **What you'll receive**
> - 5 high-resolution renders, one per variety (white and transparent versions)
> - The 3D model files (3DsMax), so you own them and can reuse them for future angles, campaigns, or new flavors
>
> **Total cost: 750 USD**
>
> To get started, I'll need the print-ready label files (or dielines) for each flavor, the exact packaging dimensions, and any photos of the physical product you have on hand, so we can match the real finish as closely as possible.
>
> If this works for you, just let me know, and I'll send over the next steps. We charge $450 upfront; the remaining balance is due after your approval and before delivery of high-res files. Happy to answer any questions in the meantime.
>
> Best regards,

Notes: thanks once, then the point. Colon to expand an idea. Bold labels, bullets, price on its own line.
