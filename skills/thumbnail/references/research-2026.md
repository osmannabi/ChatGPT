# Thumbnail research notes (gathered 2026-10-01)

How much to trust each source: **A** = platform or an established outlet reporting a platform change.
**B** = practitioner or tool-vendor blog with first-hand testing. **C** = a vendor blog citing
statistics it doesn't link to; treat these as direction, not fact.

## Primary playbook
- Higgsfield AI, *5 High-CTR Thumbnail Frameworks*, Playbook Vol. 2, 2026 (internal PDF supplied by
  the user). Frameworks: Before/After, Mid-Action Shots, Recognizable Logos, 3-Step Progression,
  Compelling Screenshots. Core test: sketch several variants, compare them side by side at feed size,
  keep the one that raises a question the title doesn't fully answer.

## Platform facts (A)
- **Test & Compare** expanded in December 2025 to titles, thumbnails, or title+thumbnail combos.
  Up to 3 variants. Desktop Studio only. Requires Advanced Features, not YPP. Runs up to 2 weeks.
  The winner is chosen by watch time share, not CTR. Sources:
  [Search Engine Journal](https://www.searchenginejournal.com/youtube-title-a-b-testing-rolls-out-globally-to-creators/562571/),
  [YouTube Help: A/B test titles and thumbnails](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/16391400?hl=en-GB),
  [vidIQ](https://vidiq.com/blog/post/youtube-launches-new-thumbnail-testing-tool/),
  [PPC Land](https://ppc.land/youtube-to-test-up-to-3-versions-of-one-video-on-real-viewers-shorts-too/).
- **Spec:** 3840x2160 recommended, 16:9. Minimum width 640 px. JPG/PNG. 50 MB from desktop, 2 MB
  from mobile. 1280x720 is still accepted. Shorts use 2160x3840. Sources:
  [thumbnailtest.com](https://thumbnailtest.com/guides/youtube-thumbnail-size/),
  [postfa.st (updated Sept 2026)](https://postfa.st/sizes/youtube/thumbnail),
  [fuser.studio](https://fuser.studio/articles/youtube-thumbnail-size). I couldn't reach YouTube's
  own spec page from this environment, so confirm it in Studio's upload dialog.

## Safe zones (B)
- The duration badge sits bottom-right, 4 px from the corner. It is about 44 px wide for a short
  duration like "3:45" and about 62 px for "1:23:45" (at 1280x720).
- A red progress bar runs along the bottom edge on videos the viewer has partly watched.
- Keep key content in the center 1100x620 (at 1280x720), roughly 8% horizontal and 10% vertical margins.
- Some mobile surfaces crop toward 4:3, which cuts the left and right edges.
- Sources: [thumix](https://www.thumix.com/blog/youtube-thumbnail-safe-zone),
  [clickyapps](https://clickyapps.com/creator/thumbnails/guides/thumbnail-safe-text-areas),
  [thumbmagic](https://www.thumbmagic.co/blog/youtube-thumbnail-size).

## Design findings (B/C, directional)
- 0-3 words of text beat longer text, and the gap is larger on mobile.
- Expressive faces lift CTR. Sources cite gains of 20-30% (C).
- Minimalist thumbnails beat cluttered ones. One source cites +18% in a "50,000 thumbnails" study (C, unlinked).
- A/B-tested thumbnails beat untested ones. One source cites up to +30% and attributes it to Tubular Labs (C).
- A consistent channel style lifts CTR from subscribers. One source cites 15-20% (C).
- Mobile accounts for roughly 60-70% of YouTube viewing (figures vary by source).
- 2026 trend, "proof of human": viewers scroll past obviously generative images (perfect skin,
  impossible lighting, uncanny eyes). Real skin texture and micro-expressions beat the
  exaggerated mouth-agape look. One source cites a 22% "click satisfaction" gain (C).
- Typical CTR: 2-10% depending on niche. Average channels sit around 3-4% and top creators around 5-10% (C).
