### Gap to know about: 35milimetre.com has NO branding or packaging page

Confirmed 2026-09-07 by reading the live nav. There is no /branding, /packaging, /design or case-study section, and the site's own meta description reads "Post-production studio delivering AI image generation, 3D rendering and premium retouching for product brands."

**Why it matters:** on a branding, identity, packaging or design-system brief — especially one that explicitly rules out AI-generated work, as healthcare and regulated-sector briefs often do — linking the 35milimetre.com homepage actively argues against the pitch. It presents Mehmet as an AI post-production supplier at the exact moment the client is screening for hand-built design craft.

**How to apply:** on Behance, LinkedIn, email and direct branding briefs, break the normal platform rule and link `https://mehmet-turan.com/branding` (or a specific Behance branding project) instead of 35milimetre.com. Name the studio as 35milimetre in the signature, but send them to the branding work. If the studio site ever gains a branding page, replace this note.
