## What the Upwork profile VISIBLY sells

AI product video and ad variants · AI product/lifestyle imagery with the real product locked · 3D product rendering, CGI and product animation · AI workflow / pipeline build · high-end product retouching, compositing and manipulation · art direction on product imagery · CPG, beverage and personal care categories.

## Real work the Upwork profile does NOT signal

Brand identity and logo · packaging and label design · illustration, character and mascot · presentation and deck design · layout, editorial and general graphic design · fashion and people-led imagery · poster and banner design · motion design · architecture.

**The gap is the profile, not the portfolio.** mehmet-turan.com has pages for most of the above (/packaging, /branding, /bottle-designs, /vector-illustration, /fashion, /ai-fashion, /banner, /motion-design, /architecture, /headshots), so the work exists and can be linked. What is missing is the signal in the headline and skills list that a client scans before deciding whether to click through. On a cold application into the second list: flag it, lead the letter with the closest thing the profile does own, link the specific portfolio page, and expect a lower open rate.
