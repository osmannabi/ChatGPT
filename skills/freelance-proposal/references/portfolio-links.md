- **`mehmet-turan.com`** — Upwork and Fiverr. Carries no contact details, so it does not breach
  either platform's rules about routing clients off-platform.
- **`35milimetre.com`** — Behance, LinkedIn, email, and any direct client. The studio site, with
  contact details and the articles at `/news`.

## Picking the right one

**Match the deliverable, not the industry.** A bottle render for a spirits brand is 3D Rendering &
Compositing, not Alcoholic Beverages; the alcohol pages are for jobs that are specifically about
beverage visuals as a category.

**For pure AI generation**, prefer `/100-ai` over `/ai`; `/ai` is product-specific.

**For compositing on a real photograph**, use `/image-compositing`. For brand or CPG product
retouch, `/product` or `/cpg`.

**Two links only when the job genuinely spans two distinct services** (3D modelling *and* label
design, for example). Most relevant first. Never more than two; a list of links reads as a brochure
and undoes the personal tone the letter is working for.

**The homepage is a fallback, not a default.** A specific page proves the relevant work exists; the
homepage makes the client do the searching, and they won't.
